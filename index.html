Meet Naveen Chandra Kukkala

Distribution Leader & Author

Naveen is Director of Distribution at EssilorLuxottica North America, where he leads warehouse operations optimization. He writes about how automation, AI and people work together in warehouses, and is the author of the forthcoming book Smart Warehousing.

Director of Distribution

Naveen has spent fourteen-plus years in operations across aerospace, logistics, retail, medical devices and eyewear — at Hindustan Aeronautics, DHL Supply Chain, Nordstrom and Medtronic before joining EssilorLuxottica in 2024. He speaks at industry conferences including CSCMP EDGE, WERC and IISE, and has been interviewed by Supply Chain Digital and Robotics Tomorrow.
